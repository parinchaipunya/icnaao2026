Scope and Topics

Original research contributions falling within the scope of nonlinear analysis and mathematical optimization are cordially invited. Suggested areas include, but are not limited to:

  • Nonlinear Functional Analysis and Fixed Point Theory
  • Convex and Non-Convex Optimization
  • Stochastic Optimization and Uncertainty Quantification
  • Algorithmic Decomposition Methods (e.g., ADMM, Lagrangian Relaxation)
  • Integer and Combinatorial Linear Programming
  • Variational Inequalities and Complementarity Problems
  • Applications in Operations Research, Economics, and Engineering Systems

Submission Guidelines

Prospective authors are invited to submit an abstract (1-2 pages) outlining their original research work via the official conference submission portal or by email to the organizing committee.
